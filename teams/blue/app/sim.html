Crews: the Field, Weeks 9 to 13

What it says

From the Week 8 cohort alone, with no new behaviour, the Field reaches 42 by Week 13. Crews on the cohort's own recruiting rate reaches 42.7, so the mechanism changes little until it changes how often people bring someone in. If the countdown doubles recruiting and a live crew keeps its members 5 points more often, the Field reaches 46.4 and clears the 40 rung in 86% of seasons (baseline 67%). The design moved because of this run: a 72-hour timer left crews cold most of the week with this cohort's recruiting, so the live rules use 7 days.

The mechanism, as simulated

  • Every crewmate adds +0.1x to every member's Prize Multiplier, up to +1.0x; a crew holds 12. Size counts members in that week's Field.
  • Alive: 3 or more members and the timer running. Someone new to Rush resets the timer to 7 days; a player from the Field moving in adds 48 hours. The boost that counts is the one held when the week settles.
  • Multiplier: the referral rule (1 + 0.1 per active referral) plus the crew boost, capped at 3x.

From the cohort file (COHORT-WEEK8.md)

  • Start: Week 8, 33 entrants (12 up or flat, 20 down, 1 with no pick) and 19 accounts outside the Field.
  • Back next week: 0.92 after a win, 0.77 after a loss, 0.4 after no pick; reactivation 0.18.
  • Signups 4.5 a week in all: personal referrals at 0.04 per entrant-week, the rest organic (3.62 a week). New players enter 0.92, first pick 0.81 (referred 1).
  • Pickers finishing up: 0.27. Ladder rungs at 40, 75, 125, 200.

Assumptions the cohort file does not support

  • Adoption 0.55 of the Field joins a crew in Week 9 (anchored on 18 of 33 Week 8 entrants who came by a friend), then 0.19 a week of those not yet in one; half the new players who come in on their own join an open crew.
  • Recruiting lift: a crew member brings someone new at the cohort's referral rate times the lift. 1.1 is the only lift the file supports (multiplier era 0.044 against 0.040 a season); 2 and 3 are assumptions, shown separately.
  • Return bonus: +5 points on coming back next week for members of a crew alive at settle. No data in the file; shown with and without.
  • Two-sided linking (founder addendum): a crew membership counts only once both sides confirm; assumed 9 in 10 requests are confirmed, within 24 hours. The baseline keeps the observed referral numbers.
  • Return pace: the main runs use the pooled return rates (0.77 after a loss, 0.92 after a win), which grow the Field about 2 a week. The file also shows 26 of 28 coming back from Week 7 to Week 8 (0.93), the pace behind its +4 a week trend; the rows marked W7 to W8 carryover rerun the baseline and Crews on that.
  • Crews form among friends in groups of 3 to 6; a player stays in their crew across weeks; joins happen through the week at random hours.

Reproduce
